# Hydraweave artwork

Original artwork for Hydraweave: three alert, angular hydra heads rise from broad ribbon necks that cross over and under one another into a single pointed plait. The three heads represent cooperating AI nodes and GPUs; the braid represents their shared inference result. The silhouette and ribbon paths were drawn by hand, following exploration of four original image-generated concepts. No existing logo was traced.

- `hydraweave-logo.svg`: horizontal lockup on white, with a 960 × 256 viewBox.
- `hydraweave-logo-dark.svg`: matching lockup on midnight indigo, with brighter indigo ribbons and pale lettering.
- `hydraweave-mark.svg`: transparent square icon, with a 256 × 256 viewBox; suitable for portal icons and small uses.
- `hydraweave-logo.png` and `hydraweave-logo-dark.png`: 512 × 137 PNG exports of the respective lockups.
- `hydraweave-mark.png`: transparent 512 × 512 icon export.
- `hydraweave-social.png`: 1280 × 640 social preview, with the dark identity and “Many heads. One inference.”

The palette uses indigo (`#5149B9`, brightened to `#8F86F1` on dark), teal (`#078D92`), and electric mint (`#3DDBB5`), with neutral backgrounds and lettering. Flat overlaps create the weaving depth. There are no gradients, filters, embedded bitmaps, masks, scripts, external resources, or live font dependencies in the SVGs.

The wordmark is Manrope at weight 750, with optical tracking, converted into explicit SVG paths. The original mark is distributed under the project license.

Keep the complete square viewBox around the icon and the complete lockup viewBox as their minimum clear space. Use the full lockup at approximately 420 px wide in the README. The icon was visually checked at 16, 32, and 64 px; use 32 px or larger when recognition of all three heads matters. The SVGs remain the editable artwork masters. PNGs were rendered with CairoSVG.
